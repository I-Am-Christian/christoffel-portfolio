About me

Junior Software Engineer with experience in Azure Cloud, web development, website hosting and Java. I have built and deployed web apps and worked with HTML, CSS, JavaScript and SQL.

I enjoy experimenting with artificial intelligence and have built my own AI assistant while exploring locally run AI, different AI models, and how they work behind the scenes. This has given me a growing understanding of AI systems and the technology that powers them.

I have a genuine love for the digital world and enjoy exploring what technology can do.
